Welcome to the **Brain-Computer Interface Handbook**!

This blog will document research, development, and insights into BCI technology.

## What is BCI?

A Brain-Computer Interface (BCI) is a system that enables direct communication between the brain and external devices.

### Key Areas

- **Neural Signal Acquisition**: EEG, fNIRS, invasive recordings
- **Signal Processing**: Filtering, feature extraction
- **Machine Learning**: Pattern recognition, classification
- **Applications**: Medical, accessibility, gaming
